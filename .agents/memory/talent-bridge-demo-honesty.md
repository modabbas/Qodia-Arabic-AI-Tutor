---
name: Talent Bridge demo honesty
description: Boundary between simulated hiring activity and real employer actions in Qodia.
---

Public employer demos may animate metrics, notifications, and candidate profiles, but must visibly identify them as simulated. An interview request, job posting, or video playback must not claim success unless a real service performed it and confirmed the outcome.

**Why:** The competition demo intentionally has no hiring backend or real candidate recordings. A convincing animation is useful, but pretending a person was contacted or a job was published would mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** Keep demo labels and explicit no-send explanations while data is mocked. When real employer workflows are introduced, require authorized access to private candidate data and replace demo messaging only after the corresponding server operation succeeds.
