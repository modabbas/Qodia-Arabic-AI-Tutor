---
name: Qodia demo honesty
description: Boundary between illustrative marketing/demo content and verified outcomes or real employer actions.
---

Public Qodia demos may animate metrics, notifications, and candidate profiles, but must visibly identify them as simulated. An interview request, job posting, or video playback must not claim success unless a real service performed it and confirmed the outcome. The user confirmed that proposed marketing study figures, usage/outcome numbers, certificate accreditation, and named testimonials are not verified; do not state or attribute them as real. Clearly label illustrative figures and scenarios, and avoid implying university partnerships.

**Why:** Competition demos intentionally lack verified hiring activity and evidence for the proposed social-proof claims. Convincing presentation is useful, but invented research, endorsements, affiliations, or completed actions would mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** Keep demo labels and explicit no-send explanations while data is mocked. Use plainly fictional learning scenarios instead of unattributed testimonial quotes. Replace illustrative marketing claims only with sourced evidence. When real employer workflows are introduced, require authorized access to private candidate data and replace demo messaging only after the corresponding server operation succeeds.
