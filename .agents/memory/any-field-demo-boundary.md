---
name: Any-field demo boundary
description: Product scope and privacy expectations for Kodia's public generalization demonstration.
---

The user defines “Try Any Field” as a public, standalone demonstration of Five Touches beyond programming for landing-page visitors, judges, and investors. It must not use accounts, Learning Path, Cumulative Project, student validation, hints, XP, or grading. Demo inputs and answers are ephemeral, without database writes.

**Why:** The goal is to show the teaching interaction in other university disciplines, not create additional student learning paths or alter the real product.

**How to apply:** Keep future additions isolated from student systems. Match the Kodia visual language through read-only reuse. Treat changes to existing student pages or engines as out of scope. Keep Touch 4 to multiple choice or ordering, never code writing; Touch 5 receives a brief, ungraded, genuine model reaction. Preserve honest failure/retry states instead of fabricated feedback.

The demonstration is educational, not professional medical or engineering advice, and its interactivity is not empirical evidence of learning outcomes.

**Why:** The demo includes clinical and engineering subjects and serves a public judging audience; fictional scenarios must not imply verified efficacy or professional qualification.

**How to apply:** Keep visible AI/educational disclaimers and avoid formal scores, clinical recommendations, accreditation, or claims of measured efficacy.
