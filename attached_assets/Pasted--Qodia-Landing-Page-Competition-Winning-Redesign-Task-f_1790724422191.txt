## Task for Replit Agent

**IMPORTANT: Enable Extended Thinking (🧠) before starting.**

Completely redesign the main landing page at `/` to be a competition-winning, professional, conversion-focused page that impresses judges from the first 7 seconds.

## The Goal

When a judge or investor opens Qodia.com, they should immediately think:
1. "This is a real, serious company" (not a student project)
